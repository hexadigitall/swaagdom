# SWAAGDOM Brand Guide

## Brand Structure
- **Platform/Product Brand:** **Swaagdom**
- **Primary Tagline:** **Style, Swaggadishly.**
- **Campaign/Editorial Tone:** **Swaggadishly**

## Brand Positioning
Swaagdom is an AI-powered fashion platform for global style discovery, inspired by African design traditions and built for modern self-expression.

## About Blurb
Swaagdom brings together intelligent styling and culturally grounded creativity to help people discover their personal style with confidence. We design for global diversity with a premium, inclusive point of view.

## Voice Attributes
- Premium and expressive
- Culturally grounded and globally fluent
- Confident and modern
- Inclusive and respectful
- Intelligent, never robotic

## Tone Guide
### Do
- Use elegant, clear language with editorial polish.
- Celebrate individuality, heritage, and contemporary culture.
- Keep AI references helpful, human, and empowering.

### Don’t
- Don’t use forced slang or gimmicky phrasing.
- Don’t overstate cultural references or stereotype audiences.
- Don’t make the tone exclusive, loud, or trend-chasing.

## Using “Swaggadishly”
- Use **Swaggadishly** as a style signal and campaign voice—not as the product name.
- Pair naturally with action words around style, confidence, and expression.
- Preferred line: **Style, Swaggadishly.**

## Example Copy Snippets
- "Discover global style, curated with intelligence and worn Swaggadishly."
- "From Lagos to London, style intelligence for every expression."
- "Dress with confidence—boldly, elegantly, Swaggadishly."
- "Swaagdom helps you find what fits your identity, not just the algorithm."
